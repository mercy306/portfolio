1. **Backdrop Filter** - Glass morphism
2. **Background Clip** - Gradient text
3. **Pseudo Elements** - Shine effects
4. **CSS Variables** - Theme consistency
5. **Radial Gradients** - Background patterns
6. **Transform & Transition** - Smooth animations
7. **Box Shadow** - Colored glows

Extensive use of:
- `bg-gradient-to-r/from/via/to`

- `transition-all` and `duration-*`
